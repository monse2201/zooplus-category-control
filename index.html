The case study

How I would approach a pet category at zooplus.

At Tobipets, I was the person who got called when a dashboard was wrong or a campaign didn't ship correctly. This case study applies that same lens — making the plan actually happen — to zooplus's Dog Food & Treats category.

Disclaimer This is an independent strategic exercise based on publicly available information and illustrative datasets. It does not use confidential zooplus data.

Why Dog Food & Treats?

It's zooplus's largest, most structurally rich category — multiple subcategories, brands, specialist diets, formats, filters and promotions. The category listing I reviewed showed 1,598 products, filterable by food type, size, flavour, packaging, price, life stage, benefits, price/kg and reviews. That's the right complexity to demonstrate category management thinking.

The core insight

The category is not just a shelf. It's a decision system. A customer doesn't think "I need SKU #348." They think "my dog is 4 years old, sensitive stomach, medium-sized, and I want something good but not too expensive." The category has to connect that need to a purchase — and bring them back.

If I started tomorrow

An 18-month contract, run in three phases.

DAYS 0–30

Learn the machine

  • Current promo calendar & CPM setup
  • Pricing framework & approval flow
  • Open tickets across CRM, Shop Ops, Product
  • FR/BE stakeholder map & reporting cadence
  • Where deviations vs. plan tend to happen
Output: Execution Health Map
DAYS 31–60

Close the gaps

  • 1 promo QA checklist in use
  • 1 price change SLA agreed with Pricing
  • 1 recurring PLP/Own Brand audit running
  • 1 backlog of open items, owned & dated
Output: A working execution tracker
DAYS 61–90

Run the cadence

  • Weekly promo & pricing status to Cluster leadership
  • Post-promo analyses delivered on schedule
  • Category KPI reporting (NS, margin, spend, availability)
  • Early flags on deviations, before they're a pattern
Output: Reliable, on-time category steering

In an 18-month contract, the win isn't a big idea in month one. It's a category that runs cleanly for all eighteen.
